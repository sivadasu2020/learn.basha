How to use this book

Your goal is to greet a Kannada speaker, introduce yourself, ask and answer simple questions, use respectful language, and recover politely when you do not understand.

Daily Session A — 30 min

Learn phrases, pronunciation and sentence patterns.

Daily Session B — 30 min

Recall without notes, speak aloud, role-play and log mistakes.

Progress rule

Pass theory at 80/100 before doing the assessed practical. Revise and retest if needed.

Day 3 — Yes, no and simple questions

Session A: Theory (30 minutes)

Step 1 — Learn these phrases (10 min).

HowduYes
IllaNo / not
Nimge Kannada barutta?Do you know/speak Kannada?
Kannada gotha?Do you know Kannada? (colloquial)
SwalpaA little

AaytuOkay / done
Yen?What?

Some forms vary by context. For polite speech, prefer a complete question such as “Nimge Kannada barutta?”

Step 2 — Question and answer (10 min).

Question: Nimge Kannada barutta?
Do you speak Kannada?

Answer 1: Howdu, swalpa barutte.
Yes, I know a little.

Answer 2: Illa, nanage Kannada chennagi baralla.
No, I don't speak Kannada well.

Step 3 — Listen and respond (10 min). Answer aloud: Nimma hesaru enu?; Hegiddira?; Nimge Kannada barutta?; Chennagiddira?; Kannada kaliyuttiddira?

For the last question, one useful reply is “Howdu, Kannada kaliyuttiddini” — Yes, I am learning Kannada.

Session B: Practice (30 minutes)

Challenge: Answer even when a question comes in an unexpected order.

Recover from a misunderstanding

Local speaker: Nimge Kannada barutta?
Do you speak Kannada?
You: Nanage Kannada swalpa barutte. Dayavittu swalpa nidhaanavaagi maatadi.
I know a little Kannada. Please speak a little slowly.
If you still don't understand: Kshamisi, nanage artha agalilla. Matte heli.
Sorry, I didn't understand. Please say it again.

Recovery sequence

  1. Listen to the sentence.
  2. If you understand, answer.
  3. If you don't understand, say “Nanage artha agalilla.”
  4. Ask “Matte heli.”
  5. If needed, request “Swalpa nidhaanavaagi maatadi.”
  6. Repeat what you understood to confirm.

Challenge: Try a clarification phrase before switching to English or Telugu.

Real-world practice guidelines

  • Choose a safe, low-pressure setting and a willing speaker.
  • Do not interrupt busy workers or treat strangers as compulsory tutors.
  • Start with a simple greeting and ask whether they have a moment.
  • Do not practise with a complicated transaction or urgent situation.
  • For money, addresses, travel destinations or health concerns, repeat and confirm important details. If misunderstanding could cause harm, use a language you both understand.
